Roof Lantern Sizes: How to Measure

Roof lantern sizes are the external size of the upstand, or kerb, that the lantern sits on, including any roof covering. They're not the size of the hole in your ceiling.

How to measure your upstand

Three quick checks before you order.

1

Measure the outside

Take the outside of the upstand, width first and then length, including any roof covering.

2

Check a few points

Measure at a few points and use the smallest figure.

3

Building a new upstand?

Size it to one of our listed sizes to keep costs down.

Width first, then length

Width is the shorter side of the upstand and length is the longer side. Ridged lanterns suit rectangular openings, and pyramid lanterns suit square ones, where width and length are the same.

  • Measure to the outside of the finished upstand, including any roof covering
  • Sizes are listed in 100mm steps
  • Sheerline S1 lanterns up to 3.2m by 6m need no tie bars
