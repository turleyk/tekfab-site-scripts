How the SheerVent works

The vents sit flush with the glass, so the lantern looks almost the same as a fixed one. They open at the touch of a button to let out heat and steam.

  • Sits flush with the rafter bars, with no extra bars across the glass
  • Electric actuator, so there are no handles or winders to reach
  • Fully thermally broken, with the same 28mm glazing as the rest of the roof
  • Rain and wind sensors that close the vents automatically are sold separately
