Roof Lantern or Skylight: What's the Difference?

A skylight is a single flat pane set into the roof. A roof lantern is a raised glazed structure that catches light from several directions.

Why choose a roof lantern?

Because the glass faces several directions, a lantern catches light throughout the day and makes a room feel taller and more open. That's why lanterns are so popular over kitchens and extensions.
